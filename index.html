📋 Requirements

  • Browser: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (latest version)
  • Device: ESP32 development board
  • Cable: USB data cable (not charge-only)
  • Connection: HTTPS website (required for Web Serial API)

🔧 Installation Steps

  1. Connect your ESP32 to your computer via USB cable
  2. Click "Upload Firmware" button above
  3. Select your device from the browser popup (usually shows as "CP210x" or "CH340")
  4. Wait for installation to complete (takes about 30 seconds)
  5. Configure WiFi when prompted by the browser
  6. Access your device via the provided web link
⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Make sure your ESP32 is not running any serial monitor during flashing
  • Some ESP32 boards may require you to hold the BOOT button during connection
  • If flashing fails, try a different USB cable or port
  • The first boot after flashing may take up to 30 seconds
